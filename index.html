    Glass-to-glass test

    The camera must see this screen (aim it at the screen, or at a mirror that shows it). The whole screen flashes black and white 20 times; this times how long until a bright frame reaches the app. Compare FaceTime HD, iPhone wireless and iPhone over USB. The flash and the camera's frame timing are included, and so is the screen's own delay (a few ms, the same for every camera).

      Right hand: fist = play/pause · index up = back to START (or 0:00) · V = clear · hold thumbs up = 2× while held, back to your speed when you let go; an open palm does nothing. Left hand: V = set START (IN) · index up = set END (OUT) and loop; it never plays or pauses by itself. Steering wheel: raise the LEFT hand (palm, V or index) with the right hand in the zone, then circle the right hand clockwise = forward (no limit). While steering, the left hand picks what moves: open palm = playhead (the music stays as it is) · fist = playhead, paused while the fist is closed (open it to a palm to resume) · V = loop START · index = loop END · shaka (thumb + pinky out) = speed: circle the right hand to step the speed by 0.025 (0.5 to 1.0×, four full circles), the music keeps playing. Keys: - / = 0.025 slower / faster, 0 = 1×, T = 2× / 1×, Shift+[ ] = 1 s nudge.
